# Takt: icon refinements — 2026-09-23

Four design candidates requested by the user; Android assets are unchanged.

- 1A Ribbon: folded T with diagonal negative space and clipped foot.
- 1B Sync: modular rounded T with a diagonal pause in its stem.
- 4A Orbital T: original orbit language combined with a tilted T.
- 4B Momentum: flowing, tapered orbit reaching towards its satellite dot. The generator simplified the proposed inward pointer to a taper; this is the actual delivered concept.

Generated using gpt-image-2 through the configured CodexLB Images API. Exact prompts are in prompts.json. The provider returned 1254px RGBA sources with matte noise rather than the requested uniform background. Original responses remain in *-source.png. Preview processing removes matte fragments and small pinholes, retains the generated shapes, normalizes the symbol to a maximum 604px dimension within 1024px, and applies white on cobalt #345DD2. No Android implementation is included.

The comparison board includes the previous 01 and 04 previews, each of the four new candidates, simulated light/dark purple recoloring, and 48px/32px samples. Purple images are design simulations, not real launcher screenshots or proof of Android integration. Final adaptive foreground/background, monochrome XML and launcher verification follow only after selection.
